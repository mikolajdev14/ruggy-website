## Design Specificity Verdict

Grounded on the outside, generic at the core. Landing layer is genuinely product-authored (tufted-field.tsx yarn-parting interaction, disciplined "Wuja Dywaniarz" voice system, stitch motifs, honest custom-price formulas). Configurator interior (customer-form, delivery-picker, date-picker, FormPanel + sticky bar) abandons Ruggy tokens for stock neutral-gray shadcn at the payment/commitment moment. Persuade layer grounded, Operate layer interchangeable — that inversion is the core finding.

Deterministic scan: 0 findings across all 16 surfaces, exit 0, validated with a positive-control canary (correctly returned 4 findings). No cheap AI tells — no gradient text, no generic palette, no bounce easing, no fake glow. Detector and design eye agree: problems are architectural (two design languages, late-revealed fork, keyboard-dead tiles), not surface-token laziness. Env note: duplicate lowercase design.md alongside DESIGN.md.

## Overall Impression

Product-specific landing craft handing off to a generic checkout. Biggest opportunity is not to add — it's to carry the identity already built (exists in size-picker.tsx) across into the money screens.

## What's Working

1. tufted-field.tsx earns its weight completely — it IS the product thesis, engineered for reduced-motion, off-screen pause, coarse-screen density scaling.
2. Copy voice is a system, not decoration — one Polish single-maker across hero, steps, upsell, success, even error strings.
3. Pricing honesty on the custom path — actual formula, "orientacyjna" label, Instagram-final caveat. Admin quietly excellent.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Visual coherence collapses in the configurator interior.** Three form components + sticky bar use stock neutral-gray instead of Ruggy tokens. Brand equity evaporates at commitment moment; violates DESIGN.md order-page rule. Fix: restyle to size-picker.tsx system (--ruggy-surface, cobalt focus rings, rounded-2xl, thread texture).

**[P1] Paid-vs-quote fork revealed too late.** rug-card.tsx shows no price/path signal; user learns fate from submit button label. Feels like bait-switch, contradicts PRODUCT.md principle #1. Fix: badge each card, add path banner atop configurator.

**[P2] Anti-slip upsell is a blocking modal at commitment — and nonsensical on quote path.** AntiSlipOfferDialog fires before submit even on Instagram path where no price exists. Fix: never show on quote path; inline checkbox on paid path.

**[P2] DomeGallery keyboard-dead and screen-reader-noisy.** ~175 tiles role=button tabIndex=0 with no onKeyDown, near-identical alt text, scroll-locks. Fix: Enter/Space activation, cut tabbable count, distinct alts, static-grid fallback.

**[P3] Dead decorative weight.** Silk/ColorBends/FloatingLines/OrbitImages imported nowhere; DomeGallery ships Unsplash + pbs.twimg.com default images. Fix: delete or wire to Ruggy content.
